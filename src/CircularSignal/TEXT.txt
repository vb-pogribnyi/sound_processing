- Cylindrical space
- Each radial point samples a plane, a result or adaptation of a single airfoil CFD or approximation.
- Horizontal axis of the plane translates to time point in the cylinder.
- The plane is rotating


- A mesh, as a np array. XxYxZxT dimensions.
- SourceDrawer, with defined coordinates, RPM, number of blades, link to PlaneReader.
- PlaneReader, having links to N planes. Takes float coordinates from 0 to 1 in both vertical and horizontal axis.
- Visualizer, for every Z plane.
